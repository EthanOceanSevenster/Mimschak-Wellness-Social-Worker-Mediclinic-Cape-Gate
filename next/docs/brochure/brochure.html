Meet your social worker

Care grounded in experience

Phakama Ndamase is a qualified and experienced social worker whose career is grounded in compassion, professionalism, and a deep commitment to improving the well-being of individuals, families and communities.

She began her career in 2007 at the Pietermaritzburg Child and Youth Care Centre. Later that year she joined the Child and Family Welfare Society of Pietermaritzburg, where she worked until 2013 in statutory social work, child protection and family reunification.

In 2013 she established her private practice and began working with ICAS, an employee wellness organisation serving government departments and the corporate sector. Since 2014 she has also provided hospital-based social work services at Medical Towers Hospital in Isipingo, Durban, and she mentors social work students as a supervisor at the University of South Africa.

She believes in creating a safe, respectful and non-judgemental space where you can express yourself, develop healthy coping strategies and work towards meaningful change.

Social work is more than a profession — it is a calling.
