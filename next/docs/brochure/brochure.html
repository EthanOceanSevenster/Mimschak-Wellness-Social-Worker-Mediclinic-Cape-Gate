Meet your social worker

Phakama Ndamase

UKZN (Honours) BA in Social Work
Practice number 089 000 0495425

Nearly two decades of experience supporting individuals, families, workplaces and communities.

Phakama Ndamase is a qualified and experienced social worker whose career is grounded in compassion, professionalism, and a deep commitment to improving the well-being of individuals, families and communities.

She began her career in 2007 at the Pietermaritzburg Child and Youth Care Centre, and then spent six years at the Child and Family Welfare Society of Pietermaritzburg in statutory social work, child protection and family reunification.

In 2013 she established her private practice and began working with ICAS, an employee wellness organisation serving government departments and the corporate sector. Since 2014 she has also provided hospital-based social work services at Medical Towers Hospital in Isipingo, Durban.

As a social work supervisor at the University of South Africa, she mentors the next generation of social workers. With every client, she creates a safe, respectful and non-judgemental space to develop healthy coping strategies and work towards meaningful change.

For Phakama, social work is more than a profession — it is a calling.

Online sessions

Counselling by video call or telephone, for support from anywhere.

Employee wellness, for employers

Counselling for employees facing workplace and personal challenges, group interventions, workplace wellness presentations, trauma support and critical-incident debriefing after robberies, accidents and other traumatic workplace events.
